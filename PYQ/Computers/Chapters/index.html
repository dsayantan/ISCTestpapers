ISC Computer Science: chapter-wise previous-year questions

Every question of the five solved Computer Science papers (Board 2024, 2025, 2025 Improvement, 2026 and CISCE Specimen 2025) sorted by the 14 units of the ISC 2027 Paper 1 syllabus: 139 slides, grouped by marks and newest paper first. The slides are the same worked solutions as the paper-wise decks, with ISC-style marks and a Concepts to recall panel. Unit 1 (Boolean Algebra) and Unit 2 (Computer Hardware) are further labelled by topic.

Papers not included: CISCE Specimen 2026 and Specimen 2027 for Computer Science are not held locally, so there are no decks for them. Marks: each paper sets 95 marks (Part I 20 + Part II 75) and a candidate answers 70, choosing two of three questions in each Part II section. The tables count marks set (all questions), so each column adds to 95. A mixed question (for instance board 2026 Q3, which has a decoder and a proof) appears in each unit it touches and its marks are split between them.

Units with no question of their own

  • Unit 4, Programming in Java (review): No question in these five papers sits only in this unit. The syllabus treats it as the review umbrella over units 5–13 (‘items 4 to 13 should be introduced almost simultaneously’), so every program question is filed under the unit whose idea it tests (Algorithms, Arrays and Strings, Recursion, Objects).
  • Unit 6, Primitive Values and Casting: No question in these five papers sits only in this unit. Wrapper classes and casting appear only inside other answers (Integer.parseInt in the Tech-number function, (char) casts in the hexadecimal function). Revise the wrapper classes (parseInt, valueOf, Character methods) from the syllabus.
