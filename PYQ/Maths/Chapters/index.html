ISC Maths: chapter-wise previous-year questions

Every question of the seven solved Maths papers (Board 2024, 2025, 2025 Improvement, 2026 and CISCE Specimen 2025, 2026, 2027) sorted by chapter: 298 slides in 14 chapters, grouped by marks and newest paper first. The slides are the same worked solutions as the paper-wise decks, with ISC-style marks and a Concepts to recall panel. Chapters follow the ISC 2027 Maths units. Chapter 14 holds linear regression and the commerce applications of calculus, which the 2024–2026 papers set in Section C but the ISC 2027 syllabus has dropped.
