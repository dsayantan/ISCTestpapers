ISC 2027 Sample Papers

Six sample papers per subject, set to the ISC 2027 syllabus on the pattern of the CISCE 2027 specimen paper (the 2025 specimen and 2026 board paper for Computer Science and English Language, which have no 2027 specimen here). The questions are the ones most likely in 2027, drawn from board papers 2017–2026, the specimen papers, the competency-focused booklets, NCERT and the NCERT Exemplar. The papers overlap on purpose, because a highly probable question is worth seeing more than once. Every paper is a PDF, and its answer key is a web page that prints each question followed by its answer.
