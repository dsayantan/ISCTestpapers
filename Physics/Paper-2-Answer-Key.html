(c)1 mark

Why is the total energy of the electron in a hydrogen atom negative? (Understand)

Answer

The electron is bound to the nucleus: energy (13·6 eV from the ground state) must be supplied to free it, and the zero of energy is taken at infinite separation.

In which bias is the Zener diode connected, and how does it keep the output at 5 V? (Understand)

Answer

In reverse bias, beyond its breakdown voltage. At breakdown the voltage across it stays almost constant at VZ over a wide range of current; any excess input voltage is dropped across the series resistor.

Zener regulator (ED09): B20 Q22(a)(ii), B25 Q20(iii), B26 1B(iv).
