State the Biot–Savart law. Using it, obtain an expression for the magnetic field at the centre of a circular coil of N turns and radius R carrying a current I. (Understand)

Answer

dB = μ04πI dl sin θr2   (vector form dB = (μ0/4π) I dl × r/r3)

Using Huygens' principle, draw the refracted wavefront when a plane wavefront is incident on a plane surface separating a rarer medium from a denser medium. Hence prove Snell's law of refraction. (Understand)

  4. The incident ray, refracted ray and the normal lie in one plane. This is Snell's law.

Why does nuclear fusion need a very high temperature? (Understand)

Answer

The nuclei are positively charged and repel; only at temperatures of the order of 107 K do they have enough kinetic energy to overcome the Coulomb barrier and come within the range of the nuclear force (hence ‘thermonuclear’).

(i)1 mark

Why is the photodiode operated in reverse bias rather than forward bias? (Understand)

Answer

In reverse bias the current is due only to the few minority carriers, so the fractional change caused by light-generated electron–hole pairs is large and easily measured; in forward bias the large majority current would swamp it.
