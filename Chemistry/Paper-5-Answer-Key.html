(C)3 marks

Read the passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

Osmosis is the flow of solvent through a semipermeable membrane from a dilute solution to a more concentrated one. The excess pressure that must be applied to the solution to stop this flow is its osmotic pressure, π = CRT. A 0·9 % (mass/volume) sodium chloride solution, called normal saline, has the same osmotic pressure as the fluid inside red blood cells, and is used for intravenous injection. A red blood cell placed in 5 % NaCl solution shrivels, while one placed in pure water swells and may burst.

Because π can be measured accurately at room temperature even for very dilute solutions, it is the method of choice for finding the molar masses of proteins and polymers. If a pressure larger than the osmotic pressure is applied to the solution, pure solvent is pushed out through the membrane; this reverse osmosis is used to desalinate sea water.

(i)1 mark

Name the type of solution that 5 % NaCl is with respect to the cell fluid, and explain why the red blood cell shrivels in it. (Understand)

Answer

5 % NaCl is hypertonic (higher osmotic pressure than the cell fluid). Water flows out of the cell by osmosis into the more concentrated salt solution, so the cell shrinks (crenation).

S11 osmosis phenomena: B25 Q21(i)(a) RBC in 5 % NaCl; S25 Q21(i); B24 1C(i)(ii) eggs.
(ii)1 mark

Give two reasons why osmotic pressure is preferred to other colligative properties for determining the molar mass of a protein. (Evaluate)

Answer

(1) It is measured at room temperature, so a protein is not denatured by heating or freezing. (2) Its magnitude is large even for very dilute solutions (e.g. 10−3 atm is easily measured where ΔTf would be below 10−4 K, too small to measure).

S1 context (★ S27 Q21(i)(a)); passage-style reasoning from B24 and S26.
(iii)1 mark

What is reverse osmosis? Name a material used as the membrane in desalination plants. (Understand)

Answer

When a pressure greater than the osmotic pressure is applied on the solution side, solvent flows from the solution to the pure solvent side: reverse osmosis. Membrane: cellulose acetate (permeable to water, not to dissolved ions).

S11: B25 Q21(i)(d) reverse osmosis; COMP Q60, Q84.
