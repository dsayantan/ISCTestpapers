(C)3 marks

Read the passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

Radioactive decay is a first order process: the number of nuclei that decay per unit time is proportional to the number present, N = N0e−λt, and the half-life t½ = 0·693/λ. Iodine-131 (t½ = 8 days) is used to treat an overactive thyroid gland, and carbon-14 (t½ = 5730 years) to date archaeological samples.

A living plant keeps the ratio of 14C to 12C in its tissues constant by photosynthesis. Once it dies, no fresh carbon-14 is taken in, and the activity of the 14C in the sample falls to one half every 5730 years.

(i)1 mark

Why is the half-life of a radioactive isotope independent of the amount of the isotope present? (Understand)

Answer

For a first order process t½ = 0·693/λ, which contains no concentration (amount) term; λ is a constant for the isotope, so the time taken to halve any amount is the same.

K11 (tier B): B23 1B(iv) t½ independent of initial concentration; passage slot has been physical chemistry in every paper 2024–26.
(ii)1 mark

What fraction of a dose of iodine-131 remains after 24 days? (Apply)

Answer

24 days = 24/8 = 3 half-lives, so the fraction left = (½)3 = 1/8 (12·5 %).

K4: S27 Q5 (I-131, 12 d / 48 d), COMP Q67(a) (8 d / 32 d).
(iii)1 mark

A piece of old wood shows a 14C activity that is 25 % of that of fresh wood. Estimate its age. (Apply)

Answer

25 % = (½)2: two half-lives have passed. Age = 2 × 5730 = 11460 years.

K4: C-14 dating in S25 1C(ii), S24 Q18(i), B25I Q5 (NCERT Unit 3).

Question 72 marks

Substantiate the following statements with reasons.

(i)1 mark

Sc3+ salts are colourless whereas Ti3+ salts are coloured. (Atomic number of Sc = 21, Ti = 22) (Understand)

Answer

Sc3+ is 3d0: no d electron, so no d–d transition and no absorption of visible light. Ti3+ is 3d1: the single electron is promoted t2g → eg by visible light, and the complementary colour (violet) is seen.

D4 colour (★ S27 1A(iii)); B25 Q20(i)(a) Ti3+/Ti4+.
(ii)1 mark

Zinc is not regarded as a transition element. (Atomic number of Zn = 30) (Understand)

Answer

Zn is [Ar]3d104s2 and Zn2+ is 3d10: the d subshell is completely filled both in the atom and in its only common ion, so it has no incompletely filled d orbital, which is what defines a transition element.

D2 (★ S27 Q7(ii) Zn, Cd, Hg); S24 Q5(ii) zinc not transition.
