(ii)1 mark

As the concentration of an electrolytic solution is increased, its __________ conductance increases but its __________ conductance decreases. (Understand)

Answer

specific, molar

Specific conductance (κ) is the conductance of 1 cm3: a more concentrated solution has more ions per cm3, so κ rises. Molar conductance Λm = 1000κ/M falls because inter-ionic attraction increases (and, for a weak electrolyte, α falls) while the number of moles per unit volume rises faster than κ.

Heritage Pre-Board 2024-25 Set 1 Q1(A)(i), verbatim apart from the word list (“equivalent” in the school paper); S242 Q1(A)(i) is the equivalent-conductivity sibling. E9 in every board 2023–26.
(iii)1 mark

Transition metal ions having __________ d-orbitals are colourless and __________ in nature. (Understand)

completely filled, diamagnetic

A d10 ion (Zn2+, Cu+) has no unpaired electron and no empty d-orbital, so no d–d transition can occur: it absorbs no visible light and all its electrons are paired.

Phenol is a hydroxy derivative of benzene. In low concentration (0·2 %) it is an antiseptic, whereas a 2 % solution is used as a disinfectant; it is also the starting material for dyes, plastics, drugs and explosives. (ii)1 mark

Haloalkanes react with KCN to give alkyl cyanides as the main product, but with AgCN they give isocyanides. Give a reason. (Understand)

Answer

KCN is largely ionic: it gives free CN− ions, an ambident nucleophile which attacks through the carbon (the C–C bond is stronger than the C–N bond), so RCN is formed. AgCN is largely covalent: the carbon is bonded to silver, so only the lone pair on nitrogen is available and the attack is through nitrogen, giving the isocyanide RNC.

C2H5Br + AgCNalc. C2H5NC + AgBr

Question 72 marks

Give reasons for the following:

(i)1 mark

The separation of zirconium (Z = 40) and hafnium (Z = 72) is difficult. (Understand)

Answer

Hf follows the lanthanoids, and the poor shielding by the 4f electrons causes the lanthanoid contraction. This cancels the normal increase in size down the group, so Zr and Hf have almost the same atomic and ionic radii (160 and 159 pm) and almost identical chemical properties.

(ii)1 mark

Copper is considered a transition element but zinc is not. (Atomic number of Cu = 29, Zn = 30) (Analyse)

Answer

A transition element has an incompletely filled d-subshell in the atom or in a common ion. Cu is 3d104s1, but its common ion Cu2+ is 3d9 (incomplete). Zn (3d104s2) and Zn2+ (3d10) have a completely filled d-subshell in all their states, so zinc is not a transition element.
